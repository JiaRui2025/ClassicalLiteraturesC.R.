这篇论文解决了什么What this thesis settles

The question

为什么同一面墙上，相邻的两块石头，一块已化成砂，另一块却几乎完好？Why, on the same wall, does one block crumble to sand while its neighbour stays intact?

1994 年，Carlos Rodríguez-Navarro 以格拉纳达与哈恩两座大教堂为对象，把「石材劣化」从一份描述性的病害清单，推进为一套可测量、可排序、可预测的成因—机制模型。论文的答案并不浪漫：石头不是被时间打败的，而是被孔隙里的水、盐和酸，按各自的孔隙结构，以不同的速率拆解的。 In 1994 Carlos Rodríguez-Navarro took two Andalusian cathedrals and moved stone decay from a descriptive inventory of damage to a measurable, rankable and predictive model of cause and mechanism. The answer is not romantic: stone is not defeated by time but dismantled — at different rates, according to its own pore structure — by water, salt and acid inside its pores.

内因Intrinsicalterability · 易损性

矿物组成、结构、孔隙系统、胶结程度、层理各向异性。它们不施加荷载，但决定石材对荷载的响应速度——即「易损性」。 Mineralogy, texture, pore system, degree of cementation, lamination anisotropy. These apply no load; they set the rate of response to load — alterability.

外因Extrinsicloading · 荷载

降水与干湿循环、冻融、盐溶液供给、SO₂ 与颗粒物污染、热应力、结构失稳。它们决定荷载的强度与节律，却不决定石材是否承受得住。 Rain and wet–dry cycles, frost, salt supply, SO₂ and particulate pollution, thermal stress, structural instability. These set the magnitude and rhythm of the load, not whether the stone can bear it.

核心命题：劣化形态是机制的宏观指纹，而机制由孔隙系统控制。因此，只要能定量刻画孔隙结构与水分行为，就能解释、排序并预测不同石材在同一环境中的不同命运。 The central claim: macroscopic decay forms are the fingerprint of mechanisms, and mechanisms are governed by the pore system. Quantify pore structure and water behaviour, and you can explain, rank and predict why different stones meet different fates in one environment.

    Conclusion

    结论并不落在「哪种石头好」，而落在一组可传递的判断上：耐久性不由孔隙率单调决定，而由胶结程度、黏土类型与层理各向异性三者共同决定；同一岩性内部，砌筑方向可以逆转命运；而保护处理若只追求防护效率、不报告色差与孔径改变的代价，就可能把劣化从表面推向内部。 The conclusion is not which stone is good, but a set of transferable judgements: durability is not monotonic in porosity but jointly set by cementation, clay type and lamination anisotropy; within one lithology, laying direction can reverse the outcome; and a treatment that reports efficiency without reporting colour shift and pore-size change may push decay from the surface inward.
